PIXEL HOOPS

Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026 · Updated October 5, 2026 for in-app purchases

Pixel Hoops is a single-player basketball game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Information we collect

None. Pixel Hoops does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

In-app purchases

You can buy packs of coaching credits inside the game. Apple processes every purchase through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, payment card or billing details. The game receives only a confirmation that a purchase went through, which it uses to add the credits to your career on your device.

Game data on your device

Your career (team name, roster, season progress and settings) is saved only on your device so you can pick up where you left off. It never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it in the game under Settings → Delete Career.

Network access

The game works fully offline. The only network activity is Apple's App Store system loading prices and completing purchases when you open the store screen.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle personal data, such as ads or accounts, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

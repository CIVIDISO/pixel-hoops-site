PIXEL HOOPS

Run the offense. Read the defense. Build a dynasty.

Pixel Hoops is a retro basketball game for iPhone. Every possession is a set play; your job is finding the open man, calling the right defense and building your team into a champion.

How to play

  • Pass: tap a teammate.
  • Shoot: press and hold, then release in the green.
  • Juke: swipe up or down.
  • Pump fake: a very quick tap.
  • Defense: pick man, zone or trap before the game and at each quarter break. Man beats guards, zone beats bigs, trap beats shooters.

Common questions

Where is my save? On your device. It isn't backed up to a server, so deleting the app deletes your career.

How do I start over? Open Settings in the game and choose Delete Career.

Can I play offline? Yes. You only need a connection to buy coaching credits.

I bought credits but didn't get them. Open the game again on the same career. Purchases that were interrupted are added the next time the game starts. If they still don't appear, contact us below with the date of the purchase.
